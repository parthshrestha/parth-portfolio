# Next: longer companion routines

These are planned scenes, not implemented animations. Keep the current corner placement; never drag props through portfolio content. Each routine needs authored entry, sustained activity, interruption/recovery and exit. Hide, pause, reduced motion and user navigation retain priority. Avoid a wardrobe/control-panel UI for casual visitors.

## 1. Couch rest — first proposed scene

After a long quiet interval, bring a small couch into the reserved corner stage, sit, adjust posture, exhale, and relax. Later: eyelids droop, head nods, a short nap. A poke causes a gentle wake-up, look around and smile; then the character gets up and puts the couch away. Use varied long holds, not repeated sit/stand loops.

Needed art: front/three-quarter couch with separate back cushion, seat and foreground armrest; sitting pelvis and bent legs; feet placements; closed relaxed expression. The standing paired-leg asset cannot bend into a convincing seated pose.

## 2. Couch doomscrolling

Reuse seated rig. Pick up a phone, occasionally thumb-scroll, pause to read, tiny eyebrow reaction, put the phone down and stretch. No real feed, network calls, tracking or sound. Mix reading holds of different lengths with infrequent thumb motion. Do not animate a frantic repeating swipe.

Needed art: phone, gripping hands and thumb layer, down-looking face/expression. Phone attaches to a hand socket. Gracefully stow it before standing.

## 3. Study desk

Set up a small desk and chair, open a book, follow a line, jot a note, pause and think, turn a page. Occasional stretch, then continue or pack up. Keep the scene away from navigation and shrink/omit furniture on narrow screens.

Needed art: back/front desk layers, chair, book/page layer, pencil, bent legs and writing hand. Correct depth order puts the forearms above the desktop and the lower body behind it.

## 4. Chair near-fall — rare gag

Only while seated: lean back gradually, chair pivots on rear feet, balance wobbles, eyes widen, arms counterbalance, feet catch the floor, sheepish smile. Return to the seated pose. Make this rare (minutes apart, never repeated consecutively), interruptible and entirely disabled under reduced motion.

Needed art: side/three-quarter seated pose and chair, separate chair/body pivots, leg extension and surprised face. No fall off-screen, navigation movement, sound or screen shake.

## Rollout

Polish standing motion first. Then couch sit/stand, couch nap, phone routine, study desk, and finally near-fall. Furniture scenes should occupy a bounded corner stage only when enough room exists. Mobile initially keeps the standing character. Use weighted long-duration decisions with recent-scene history; do not schedule each scene on a fixed repeating timer.
